## D3SEM: main_semantics
D3SEM	⊢ stdin_content fs = SOME text ∧ wfcl cl ∧ wfFS fs ∧ STD_streams fs ⇒
  sem_satisfies
    (semantics_dec_list (init_state (basis_ffi ext cl fs)) init_env
       delta3_prog)
    (λbehavior.
       ∃io_events.
         behavior = Terminate Success io_events ∧
         extract_fs ext (cl,fs) io_events =
         SOME (add_stdout (fastForwardFD fs 0) (d3_main (implode text))))
